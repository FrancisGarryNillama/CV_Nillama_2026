Cred-It — Automated Credit Evaluation
Document Intelligence
Python · Django 4.2 · Django REST Framework · EasyOCR · OpenCV · PostgreSQL · Redis · React · Docker Compose · pytest
Lead author, team of five. Replaced a manual credit-evaluation process for the CIT-U College of Computer Studies. A student uploads a Transcript of Records, EasyOCR + OpenCV extract it into structured subject/grade/unit data, and a similarity matcher fuzzy-matches those subjects against the department's curricula to propose credit — through a three-stage workflow (Request → Pending → Finalised) with a side-by-side transcript-versus-curriculum view for the evaluating faculty member.
  • Handles noisy, inconsistent real transcript formats, not clean synthetic input
  • Measured with 39 real users (students and faculty), not demoed — a full usability study rather than a demo day
  • System Usability Scale 73.4, above the 68 benchmark; most students completed core tasks inside 10 minutes, and faculty rated the side-by-side comparison view the most useful part of the system
  • What the evidence changed: OCR was also the single largest source of user frustration — extraction errors in subject codes, grades and units cost more trust than the automation saved. The conclusion: extraction belongs in the system as a decision-support step with visible confidence and easy manual correction, not an autonomous one. Same shape as the human-review gate in Nexus.
